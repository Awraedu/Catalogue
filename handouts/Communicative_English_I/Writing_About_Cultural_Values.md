# Writing About Cultural Values

> **Core idea:** Writing about cultural values means describing important principles or practices in a community, explaining why they matter, and showing how they influence people's behavior and society.

## 1. What Are Cultural Values?

**Cultural values** are the principles, beliefs, and ideals that a community considers important.

They can influence:

* How people behave
* How people treat others
* Family relationships
* Community relationships
* Social responsibilities
* Traditions and customs
* Attitudes toward work and education

Examples may include:

* Respect for elders
* Hospitality
* Cooperation
* Honesty
* Responsibility
* Helping others
* Community solidarity
* Respect for guests

> **Important:** Ethiopia is culturally diverse. A value or practice common in one community may not be shared by every Ethiopian.

---

# 2. What Should You Write About?

When writing about a cultural value, answer three basic questions:

### 1. What is the value?

> What principle or practice are you describing?

### 2. How is it expressed?

> How do people demonstrate this value in everyday life?

### 3. Why is it important?

> How does it benefit individuals or society?

### Simple structure

> **Value → Example → Importance**

---

# 3. Choosing a Cultural Value

Choose a value that you can explain clearly.

For example:

### Respect for elders

You could discuss:

* What respect means
* How young people show respect
* Why elders are respected
* How this strengthens relationships

### Hospitality

You could discuss:

* Welcoming guests
* Sharing food
* Helping visitors
* Building relationships

### Cooperation

You could discuss:

* Working together
* Helping neighbors
* Solving community problems
* Strengthening social relationships

---

# 4. Paragraph Structure

A good paragraph normally has **three main parts**:

### 1. Topic sentence

Introduces the main idea.

> **Hospitality is an important cultural value in many Ethiopian communities.**

### 2. Supporting sentences

Give explanations and examples.

> People often welcome guests warmly and offer them food or drink. This practice helps visitors feel respected and strengthens relationships between families and communities.

### 3. Concluding sentence

Restates the importance of the idea.

> **Therefore, hospitality can help create stronger and more supportive communities.**

---

# 5. The Basic Paragraph Pattern

Use:

> **Topic sentence → Explanation → Example → Importance → Conclusion**

### Example

> **Respect for elders is an important cultural value in many Ethiopian communities.** Young people may show respect by listening carefully to older people, using polite language, and seeking their advice. Elders are often valued because of their experience and knowledge. This respect can strengthen relationships between generations and help younger people learn from older members of the community. **For these reasons, respect for elders can contribute to social harmony and cooperation.**

---

# 6. Use Specific Examples

Avoid making your paragraph too general.

### Weak

> Hospitality is important because it is good.

### Better

> Hospitality is expressed when families welcome visitors, offer them food or coffee, and make them feel comfortable.

The second sentence gives the reader a **clear example**.

---

# 7. Explain Importance

Don't simply say that a cultural value is important.

Explain **why**.

### Weak

> Cooperation is important.

### Better

> Cooperation is important because it allows community members to share responsibilities, solve problems, and support one another during difficult situations.

### Useful expressions

* This is important because...
* It helps...
* It allows people to...
* As a result...
* Therefore...
* This contributes to...
* This strengthens...

---

# 8. Use Appropriate Academic Language

When writing for university, use clear and reasonably formal English.

### Informal

> People are really nice to guests.

### Academic

> People often demonstrate hospitality by welcoming guests and offering them food and refreshments.

### Informal

> Everyone helps each other out.

### Academic

> Community members often support one another during difficult situations.

> **Academic writing should be clear and natural—not unnecessarily complicated.**

---

# 9. Avoid Overgeneralization

Culture is diverse.

Avoid statements such as:

> **All Ethiopians always...**

> **Every Ethiopian believes...**

> **Ethiopian people never...**

Instead, use:

> **Many Ethiopian communities...**

> **In some Ethiopian communities...**

> **A common practice in some communities is...**

> **This value is particularly important in...**

This makes your writing more accurate and respectful.

---

# 10. Use Examples Carefully

Examples make a paragraph stronger.

### Example

> **Community cooperation is an important value in many Ethiopian communities.** People may work together during community activities, help neighbors with difficult tasks, and support families during important social events. Such cooperation reduces individual burdens and strengthens relationships among community members. **Therefore, cooperation contributes to social unity and mutual support.**

Notice the pattern:

> **Value → Examples → Effects → Importance**

> **This value refers to...**

> **It involves...**

> **People demonstrate this value by...**

### Giving an example

> **For example, ...**

> **One example is...**

> **This is important because...**

> **It helps to...**

> **Therefore, ...**

# 13. A Simple Writing Plan

Before writing, make five quick notes.

| Question           | Your notes                   |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- |

| What does it mean? | Welcoming and helping guests |
| Example?           | Offering food/coffee         |
| Why important?     | Builds relationships         |

Then turn the notes into a paragraph.

# 14. Model Paragraph

### Cultural Value: Hospitality

> **Hospitality is an important cultural value in many Ethiopian communities.** It involves welcoming guests warmly and making them feel comfortable and respected. For example, families may invite visitors into their homes and offer them food or coffee. Hospitality can strengthen relationships because it creates opportunities for people to communicate, share experiences, and support one another. It also helps visitors feel accepted within the community. **Therefore, hospitality is more than simply welcoming a guest; it can contribute to friendship, mutual respect, and social unity.**

---

# 15. Another Model: Cooperation

> **Cooperation is an important cultural value that can strengthen communities.** It means working together to achieve a common goal or help one another. In some Ethiopian communities, people cooperate during community activities, social events, and difficult situations. For example, neighbors may work together to complete tasks that would be difficult for one person to handle alone. Such cooperation reduces individual burdens and strengthens relationships among community members. **As a result, cooperation can promote solidarity, responsibility, and mutual support.**

---

# 16. A Strong Paragraph Formula

For exams and assignments, remember:

> **T — E — E — I — C**

### **T — Topic**

Introduce the cultural value.

### **E — Explain**

Explain what it means.

### **E — Example**

Give a specific example.

### **I — Importance**

Explain its benefit to individuals or society.

### **C — Conclusion**

End by reinforcing the main idea.

### Example

> **Hospitality is an important cultural value.**
> ↓
> **It involves welcoming and respecting guests.**
> ↓
> **For example, families may offer visitors food or coffee.**
> ↓
> **This strengthens relationships and creates social bonds.**
> ↓
> **Therefore, hospitality contributes to community unity.**

---

# 17. Common Mistakes

### ❌ Listing values without explaining them

> Respect, honesty, cooperation, hospitality, responsibility.

This is only a list.

### ✅ Explain one or two values properly.

---

### ❌ Giving no examples

> Hospitality is important.

### ✅

> Hospitality is expressed by welcoming guests and offering them food or refreshments.

---

### ❌ Making extreme claims

> All Ethiopians believe the same things.

### ✅

> Many Ethiopian communities place strong importance on hospitality.

---

### ❌ Repeating the same idea

> Hospitality is important. It is very important. It is important to society.

### ✅

Explain **how and why** it matters.

---

### ❌ Writing disconnected sentences

### ✅ Use linking words to create a logical flow.

# Quick Check

### 1. What is a cultural value?

**Answer:** A principle, belief, or ideal considered important by a community.

### 2. What are the three main things you should explain?

**Answer:** What the value is, how it is expressed, and why it is important.

### 3. Why are examples important?

**Answer:** They make the paragraph specific and support the main idea.

### 4. Which is better?

A. All Ethiopians always value hospitality.
B. Hospitality is an important value in many Ethiopian communities.

**Answer:** **B**

### 5. What should a topic sentence do?

**Answer:** Introduce the main idea of the paragraph.

### 6. Complete:

> **Value → Example → ______**

**Answer:** **Importance**

### 7. What should the conclusion do?

**Answer:** Reinforce the main idea and leave the reader with the importance of the cultural value.

### 8. Write one sentence explaining the importance of cooperation.

**Possible answer:**

> **Cooperation is important because it allows people to share responsibilities and support one another.**
