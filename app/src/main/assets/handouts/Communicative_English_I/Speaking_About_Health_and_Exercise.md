# Speaking About Health and Exercise

> **Core idea:** Speaking about health and exercise means explaining healthy habits, describing the benefits of physical activity, giving advice, and expressing opinions clearly and confidently.

## 1. Talking About Health

**Health** is a person's physical, mental, and social well-being.

When speaking about health, you can discuss:

* Physical fitness
* Exercise
* Nutrition
* Sleep
* Mental well-being
* Stress
* Healthy habits
* Illness and prevention

### Useful expressions

> **Being healthy is important because...**

> **A healthy lifestyle includes...**

> **One way to stay healthy is...**

> **Regular exercise can help...**

---

# 2. What Is Exercise?

**Exercise** is physical activity done to improve or maintain health and fitness.

Examples include:

* Walking
* Running
* Cycling
* Swimming
* Football
* Basketball
* Dancing
* Strength training
* Stretching

### Example

> **I try to exercise regularly because it helps me stay active and reduces stress.**

---

# 3. Types of Exercise

### Aerobic exercise

Activities that increase heart rate and breathing.

> Running, walking, swimming, cycling

### Strength exercise

Activities that develop muscle strength.

> Weight training, push-ups, squats

### Flexibility exercise

Activities that improve the ability of muscles and joints to move.

> Stretching, yoga

### Balance exercise

Activities that improve stability and coordination.

> Balance exercises, certain forms of yoga

> **Different types of exercise provide different benefits.**

---

# 4. Talking About the Benefits of Exercise

Exercise can provide many benefits.

### Physical benefits

* Improves cardiovascular fitness
* Strengthens muscles and bones
* Improves endurance
* Helps maintain a healthy body weight
* Increases energy
* Supports overall physical health

### Mental benefits

* Reduces stress
* Improves mood
* Supports better sleep
* Can improve concentration
* Helps people feel more energetic

### Social benefits

* Encourages teamwork
* Builds communication skills
* Creates opportunities to meet people
* Develops discipline and cooperation

The FLEn 1011 module specifically asks students to discuss the **benefits of health and fitness** as part of Unit 2.

---

# 5. Giving Your Opinion

When discussing health and exercise, you may need to express your opinion.

### Useful expressions

> **I think exercise is important because...**

> **In my opinion,...**

> **I believe that...**

> **Personally, I think...**

> **From my point of view,...**

### Example

> **In my opinion, regular exercise is important for students because it can improve both physical health and concentration.**

---

# 6. Giving Reasons

Don't stop after stating an opinion.

### Weak

> Exercise is important.

### Better

> Exercise is important **because it improves physical fitness**.

### Useful connectors

* because
* since
* as
* therefore
* so
* as a result

### Example

> Students should exercise regularly **because** physical activity can reduce stress.

---

# 7. Giving Examples

Examples make your speaking clearer.

Useful expressions:

> **For example,...**

> **For instance,...**

> **One example is...**

> **Such as...**

### Example

> Exercise can improve fitness. **For example**, running and cycling can improve cardiovascular endurance.

---

# 8. Giving Advice About Health

You may need to tell someone how to live more healthily.

### Useful expressions

> **You should...**

> **You shouldn't...**

> **You ought to...**

> **You had better...**

> **I recommend...**

> **It is a good idea to...**

### Examples

> You **should exercise regularly**.

> You **should get enough sleep**.

> You **shouldn't skip meals regularly**.

> You **ought to drink enough water**.

> It is a good idea to **take regular breaks from studying**.

---

# 9. Talking About Healthy Habits

Useful expressions:

> **I usually...**

> **I try to...**

> **I make sure to...**

> **I avoid...**

> **I regularly...**

### Examples

> I usually **walk to campus**.

> I try to **eat a balanced diet**.

> I make sure to **get enough sleep**.

> I avoid **spending too much time sitting**.

> I regularly **play football with my friends**.

---

# 10. Talking About Your Exercise Routine

You can describe your routine using the **Present Simple**.

### Example

> I exercise three times a week. I usually walk or run in the morning. At the weekend, I sometimes play football with my friends. Exercise helps me stay active and reduces stress.

### Useful questions

> **How often do you exercise?**

> **What type of exercise do you do?**

> **When do you usually exercise?**

> **Why do you exercise?**

> **How does exercise make you feel?**

# 12. Agreeing and Disagreeing

Health discussions often involve opinions.

### Agreeing

> **I agree.**

> **I completely agree.**

> **That's a good point.**

> **I think so too.**

### Disagreeing politely

> **I see your point, but...**

> **I'm not sure I agree.**

> **I understand what you mean, but...**

> **I have a different opinion.**

### Example

> **A:** Exercise is more important than diet.

> **B:** I see your point, but I think both exercise and a balanced diet are important.

---

# 13. Comparing Healthy and Unhealthy Habits

### Healthy habits

> Regular exercise

> Eating a balanced diet

> Getting enough sleep

> Drinking enough water

> Managing stress

> Staying physically active

### Unhealthy habits

> Lack of physical activity

> Poor diet

> Too little sleep

> Excessive stress

> Spending too much time sitting

### Speaking pattern

> **Healthy habit → Benefit**

> Regular exercise → improves fitness

> Enough sleep → improves concentration

> Balanced diet → provides essential nutrients

# 14. Health and Exercise for Students

University students may have:

* Long study hours
* Academic stress
* Irregular sleep
* Limited physical activity
* Busy schedules

A healthy routine can help students maintain both physical and mental well-being.

### Example

> **Students should make time for physical activity even when they have busy academic schedules. A short walk or workout can provide a break from studying and help reduce stress.**

Health is not only physical.

Useful expressions:

> **Exercise can reduce stress.**

> **Getting enough sleep can improve concentration.**

> **Taking breaks can help prevent excessive stress.**

> **Talking to someone can help when you feel overwhelmed.**

> **Maintaining social relationships can support well-being.**

# 16. Asking and Answering Questions

### Question

> **Why is exercise important?**

### Answer

> Exercise is important because it improves physical fitness, can reduce stress, and supports overall health.

### Question

> **How often should students exercise?**

### Answer

> Students should include regular physical activity in their routines. Even activities such as walking can help them stay active.

### Question

> **What is one healthy habit you have?**

### Answer

> One healthy habit I have is walking regularly. It helps me stay active and gives me a break from studying.

---

# 17. A Strong Speaking Structure

Use:

> **O → R → E → B**

### **O — Opinion**

What do you think?

### **R — Reason**

Why?

### **E — Example**

Can you give an example?

### **B — Benefit**

What is the result or benefit?

### Example

> **Opinion:** I think exercise is essential for students.
>
> **Reason:** It helps them maintain physical and mental health.
>
> **Example:** For example, students can walk, run, or play sports.
>
> **Benefit:** These activities can improve fitness and reduce stress.

This structure makes a short answer sound complete and organized.

# 20. Common Speaking Mistakes

### ❌ “Exercise make us healthy.”

### ✅ “Exercise **makes** us healthy.”

---

### ❌ “I am exercise every day.”

### ✅ “I **exercise** every day.”

---

### ❌ “I do sport every day” *(when referring generally to physical activity)*

### ✅ “I **exercise** every day.”

or

### ✅ “I **play sports** every day.”

---

### ❌ “Exercise is good because it is healthy.”

This repeats the same idea.

### ✅

> Exercise is beneficial because it improves fitness and can reduce stress.

---

### ❌ Giving only one-word answers

> “Yes.”

### ✅ Expand your answer:

> “Yes, I exercise regularly because it helps me stay active and manage stress.”

## ⭐ Exam Focus

The Ethiopian FLEn 1011 Unit 2 objective is not simply to know health vocabulary; students are expected to **discuss health and fitness in English**.

For a speaking question, use:

> **OPINION → REASON → EXAMPLE → BENEFIT**

### Example

**Question:**

> Why is exercise important for university students?

**Strong answer:**

> **In my opinion, exercise is important for university students because it supports both physical and mental health. For example, students can walk, run, or play sports regularly. As a result, they can improve their fitness and reduce stress.**

### Remember these expressions:

> **In my opinion...**

> **I believe that...**

> **This is important because...**

> **For example...**

> **As a result...**

> **Students should...**

> **I agree because...**

> **I see your point, but...**
