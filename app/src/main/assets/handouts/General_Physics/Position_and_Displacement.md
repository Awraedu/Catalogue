# Position and Displacement

Understanding where an object is and how it moves is the foundation of kinematics.

## 1. Position
**Position** is the location of an object relative to a chosen reference point, often called the origin. In one dimension, we typically use the x-axis.

## 2. Displacement
**Displacement** ($\Delta x$) is defined as the change in position of an object. It is a **vector quantity**.

### Formula
$$ \Delta x = x_f - x_i $$
Where:
- $x_f$ is the final position
- $x_i$ is the initial position

## 3. Difference between Distance and Displacement
- **Distance** is a scalar; it is the total path length traveled.
- **Displacement** is a vector; it only depends on the start and end points.

*Example*: If you walk 5m East and 5m West, your distance is 10m, but your displacement is 0m.
