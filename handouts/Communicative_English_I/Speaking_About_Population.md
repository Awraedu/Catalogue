# Speaking About Population

> **Core idea:** Speaking about population means describing how many people live in a place, where they live, how populations change, and the factors that influence population growth and distribution.

## 1. What Is Population?

**Population** is the total number of people living in a particular place at a particular time.

A population may refer to:

* A village
* A city
* A region
* A country
* The world

### Example

> **Ethiopia has a large and diverse population distributed across different regions.**

When discussing population, useful questions include:

> How many people live there?

> Where do they live?

> How densely populated is the area?

> Is the population increasing or decreasing?

> What factors affect population distribution?

---

# 2. Talking About Population Size

Useful expressions:

> **The population of Ethiopia is...**

> **The population has increased...**

> **The area has a large population.**

> **It is one of the most populated areas.**

> **The population is relatively small compared with...**

### Example

> **The population of a city may increase rapidly when people move there for education and employment.**

---

# 3. Population Growth

**Population growth** refers to an increase in the number of people living in an area.

Population can grow because of:

* High birth rates
* Lower death rates
* Immigration
* Improved healthcare
* Better living conditions

### Useful expressions

> **The population is growing rapidly.**

> **The population has increased significantly.**

> **The growth rate is relatively high.**

> **The population is expected to increase.**

### Example

> **Rapid population growth can increase the demand for housing, education, healthcare, and employment.**

---

# 4. Population Density

**Population density** describes how many people live in a particular unit of land area.

### Formula

> **Population density = Total population ÷ Land area**

For example:

> A town has 100,000 people and covers 500 km².

> **Density = 100,000 ÷ 500 = 200 people/km²**

### Useful expressions

> **The area has a high population density.**

> **The region is sparsely populated.**

> **Population density is higher in urban areas.**

> **The area has a low population density.**

---

# 5. High and Low Population Density

### High density

Many people live in a relatively small area.

Common characteristics:

* Large cities
* Many houses and buildings
* More services
* Greater demand for resources
* Heavy traffic may occur

### Low density

Fewer people live over a relatively large area.

Common characteristics:

* Rural areas
* Large open spaces
* Fewer settlements
* Greater distances between communities

### Comparison

> **Urban areas are often more densely populated than rural areas.**

---

# 6. Population Distribution

**Population distribution** refers to **where people live and how they are spread across an area**.

Population is not usually distributed evenly.

Some areas may have:

> **High concentration → many people**

Other areas may have:

> **Low concentration → few people**

### Example

> **People tend to concentrate in areas with favorable living conditions, employment opportunities, transportation, and access to services.**

---

# 7. Factors Affecting Population Distribution

Several factors influence where people live.

### Physical factors

* Climate
* Relief/topography
* Water availability
* Soil
* Natural resources

### Human factors

* Employment
* Education
* Healthcare
* Transportation
* Security
* Industry
* Government policies

### Example

> **People are more likely to settle in areas with reliable water supplies, good transportation, employment opportunities, and access to services.**

---

# 8. Urban and Rural Population

### Urban population

People living in:

* Cities
* Towns
* Urban settlements

Urban areas often have:

* More employment opportunities
* Better access to services
* Greater population density

### Rural population

People living in:

* Villages
* Countryside
* Agricultural areas

Rural areas often have:

* Lower population density
* More agricultural activity
* Greater distances between settlements

### Speaking example

> **Urban populations are often more densely concentrated, while rural populations tend to be more dispersed.**

---

# 9. Population and Migration

**Migration** is the movement of people from one place to another.

People may move because of:

* Employment
* Education
* Conflict
* Drought
* Better services
* Family reasons
* Economic opportunities

### Useful expressions

> **People migrate in search of better opportunities.**

> **Migration can change the population size of both the area people leave and the area they move to.**

> **Rural-to-urban migration can contribute to urban population growth.**

---

### Increase

### Decrease

### Stability

> remain stable

> **The population increased rapidly.**

> **The population has declined slightly.**

> **The population remained relatively stable.**

### Comparing

---

# 12. Describing a Population Pyramid

A **population pyramid** is a graph that shows the distribution of a population by **age and sex**.

It commonly has:

* **Age groups** on the vertical axis
* **Population size/percentage** on the horizontal axis
* **Males** on one side
* **Females** on the other side

### Useful speaking expressions

> **The population pyramid has a broad base.**

> **There is a large proportion of young people.**

> **The older population is relatively small.**

> **The male and female populations are fairly similar.**

# 14. Giving Your Opinion

Population issues often require discussion and opinions.

Useful expressions:

> **In my opinion,...**

> **I think that...**

> **I believe that...**

> **From my point of view,...**

> **Personally, I think...**

### Example

> **In my opinion, rapid population growth can create challenges if housing, education, healthcare, and employment do not grow at the same rate.**

---

# 15. Giving Reasons and Examples

A strong speaking answer should not stop with an opinion.

Use:

> **Opinion → Reason → Example → Result**

### Example

> **I think population growth needs careful planning because it increases demand for public services. For example, rapidly growing cities may need more schools and healthcare facilities. As a result, effective urban planning becomes important.**

Useful connectors:

* because
* since
* for example
* therefore
* as a result
* however
* in addition
* while

---

# 16. Discussing Population Problems

Population changes can create challenges when resources and services cannot keep up.

Possible issues include:

* Unemployment
* Housing shortages
* Traffic congestion
* Pressure on schools
* Pressure on healthcare
* Environmental degradation
* Food and water demand
* Urban overcrowding

### Example

> **Rapid urban population growth can put pressure on housing, transportation, education, and healthcare services.**

---

# 17. Discussing Population Opportunities

Population growth can also create opportunities.

A growing population can provide:

* A larger workforce
* More consumers
* More ideas and skills
* Greater economic activity
* Opportunities for innovation

### Example

> **A large young population can contribute to economic development if young people have access to education, skills training, and employment.**

> **Population change is not automatically a problem; its effects depend on how societies manage it.**

# 20. A Strong Speaking Structure

For most population questions, use:

> **D → E → R → I**

### **D — Define**

Explain the key concept.

### **E — Explain**

Give more information.

### **R — Reason/Example**

Support your answer.

### **I — Implication**

Explain the result or importance.

### Example: Population Density

> **Population density is the number of people living per unit of land area. It shows how concentrated a population is. For example, cities usually have higher density because many people live in a relatively small area. Therefore, densely populated areas often require more housing and public services.**

---

# 21. Common Speaking Mistakes

### ❌ “Population density means many people.”

### ✅ “Population density is the number of people living per unit of land area.”

---

### ❌ “The population are increasing.”

### ✅ “The population **is increasing**.”

> **Population** is normally treated as a singular collective noun when referring to the population as a whole.

---

### ❌ “People is moving to cities.”

### ✅ “People **are moving** to cities.”

---

### ❌ “Population is increasing because there are many people.”

This does not explain the cause.

### ✅

> “Population is increasing because of factors such as high birth rates, lower mortality, and migration.”

---

### ❌ “All people live in cities.”

### ✅

> “A large proportion of the population lives in urban areas.”

Avoid unsupported generalizations.

## Quick Check

### 1. What is population?

**Answer:** The total number of people living in a particular area at a particular time.

### 2. What is population density?

**Answer:** The number of people living per unit of land area.

### 3. Complete the formula:

> Population density = Population ÷ ______

**Answer:** **Land area**

### 4. Which area is usually more densely populated?

A. A large remote rural area
B. A major city

**Answer: B — A major city**

### 5. What is population distribution?

**Answer:** The way people are spread across a particular area.

### 6. Give two factors affecting population distribution.

**Answer:** Climate, water availability, employment, transportation, education, healthcare, and natural resources.

### 7. What is migration?

**Answer:** The movement of people from one place to another.

### 8. Complete:

> The population ______ rapidly.

A. increase
B. increases
C. increasing

**Answer: B — increases**

### 9. Give one possible challenge of rapid population growth.

**Answer:** Pressure on housing, education, healthcare, employment, infrastructure, or natural resources.

### 10. Give one possible opportunity created by a large young population.

**Answer:** A larger workforce and greater potential for economic development, provided that education and employment opportunities are available.
