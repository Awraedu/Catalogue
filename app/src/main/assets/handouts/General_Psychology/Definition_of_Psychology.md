# Definition of Psychology

Psychology is formally defined as the **scientific study of behavior and mental processes**.

## Key Components
1. **Scientific Study**: Psychology uses systematic methods (observation, experimentation) to gather and analyze data.
2. **Behavior**: Refers to any observable action (e.g., walking, talking, crying).
3. **Mental Processes**: Refers to internal, private experiences that cannot be observed directly (e.g., thinking, dreaming, feeling).

## Why is it a Science?
Psychology follows the scientific method to ensure that conclusions are based on evidence rather than intuition or common sense.
