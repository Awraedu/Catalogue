# Health and Fitness Vocabulary

> **Core idea:** Health and fitness vocabulary helps you describe **physical condition, exercise, nutrition, healthy habits, and well-being** clearly and accurately.

## 1. Health and Well-being

| Word           | Meaning                                         | Example                                |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Health**     | Overall state of physical and mental well-being | Good health is important for students. |
| **Well-being** | A healthy and comfortable state                 | Exercise improves well-being.          |
| **Illness**    | A condition that makes someone unhealthy        | He was absent because of illness.      |
| **Disease**    | A particular medical condition                  | Some diseases can be prevented.        |
| **Symptom**    | A sign of an illness or health problem          | Fever can be a symptom of illness.     |
| **Recovery**   | Return to health after illness or injury        | She made a quick recovery.             |
| **Healthy**    | In good physical or mental condition            | A healthy lifestyle is important.      |
| **Unhealthy**  | Harmful to health                               | Smoking is an unhealthy habit.         |

---

## 2. Exercise and Fitness

| Word            | Meaning                                             | Example                            |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Exercise**    | Physical activity done to improve health or fitness | I exercise three times a week.     |
| **Workout**     | A period of planned exercise                        | I had a good workout today.        |
| **Fitness**     | Ability to perform physical activities effectively  | Regular exercise improves fitness. |
| **Strength**    | Ability of muscles to produce force                 | Weight training develops strength. |
| **Endurance**   | Ability to continue an activity for a long time     | Running improves endurance.        |
| **Flexibility** | Ability to move easily through a range of motion    | Stretching improves flexibility.   |
| **Stamina**     | Physical or mental ability to continue an activity  | Athletes need good stamina.        |
| **Warm-up**     | Light exercise done before harder activity          | Always warm up before exercising.  |
| **Stretch**     | To extend or loosen muscles                         | Stretch after your workout.        |

---

## 3. Nutrition and Food

| Word              | Meaning                                                   | Example                               |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Nutrition**     | The process of obtaining nutrients from food              | Good nutrition supports health.       |
| **Nutrient**      | A substance needed by the body                            | Fruits provide important nutrients.   |
| **Protein**       | Nutrient important for growth and tissue repair           | Eggs are a source of protein.         |
| **Carbohydrate**  | Nutrient that provides energy                             | Rice contains carbohydrates.          |
| **Fat**           | Nutrient that provides energy and supports body functions | Some fats are essential for health.   |
| **Vitamin**       | Organic nutrient needed for normal body functions         | Fruits are rich in vitamins.          |
| **Mineral**       | Essential inorganic nutrient                              | Calcium is an important mineral.      |
| **Balanced diet** | A diet containing a suitable variety of nutrients         | A balanced diet supports good health. |
| **Calorie**       | A unit used to measure energy in food                     | Physical activity uses calories.      |

| Verb + Word                   | Example                                      |

| **drink water**               | Try to drink enough water.                   |
| **eat a balanced diet**       | We should eat a balanced diet.               |
| **do exercise**               | Regular exercise improves fitness.           |
| **maintain a healthy weight** | Exercise can help maintain a healthy weight. |
| **manage stress**             | Students need to manage stress effectively.  |
| **avoid unhealthy habits**    | Avoid unhealthy habits such as smoking.      |

## 5. Fitness Verbs

Useful verbs for describing physical activity:

* **exercise** → exercise regularly
* **train** → train for a competition
* **stretch** → stretch your muscles
* **strengthen** → strengthen your muscles
* **improve** → improve your fitness
* **increase** → increase your endurance
* **maintain** → maintain your fitness
* **reduce** → reduce stress
* **prevent** → prevent health problems
* **recover** → recover from exercise

### Examples

> Regular exercise **improves** fitness.
> Stretching **increases** flexibility.
> Exercise can **reduce** stress.
> Healthy habits can **prevent** some health problems.

## Quick Check

1. What is the difference between **health** and **fitness**?
   **Answer:** Health refers to overall well-being; fitness refers mainly to the ability to perform physical activities.

2. What does **endurance** mean?
   **Answer:** The ability to continue physical activity for a period of time.

3. Which word means a substance needed by the body?
   **Answer:** Nutrient.

4. Complete: Students should ______ enough sleep.
   **Answer:** get.

5. Complete: Regular exercise can ______ stress.
   **Answer:** reduce.
