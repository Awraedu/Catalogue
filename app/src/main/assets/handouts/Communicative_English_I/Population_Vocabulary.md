# Population Vocabulary

> **Core idea:** Population vocabulary includes words and **collocations** used to describe the size, growth, distribution, structure, and movement of people in a particular area.

## 1. Key Population Words

| Word                        | Meaning                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Population**              | The number of people living in a particular area                   |
| **Population density**      | The number of people per unit of land                              |
| **Population distribution** | How people are spread across an area                               |
| **Population growth**       | An increase in the number of people                                |
| **Population decline**      | A decrease in the number of people                                 |
| **Population structure**    | The characteristics of a population, especially age and sex        |
| **Population pyramid**      | A graph showing population by age and sex                          |
| **Birth rate**              | The number of births in a population over a given period           |
| **Death rate**              | The number of deaths in a population over a given period           |
| **Migration**               | Movement of people from one place to another                       |
| **Immigration**             | Moving into a country or area                                      |
| **Emigration**              | Moving out of a country or area                                    |
| **Urbanization**            | The growth of towns and cities and the increasing urban population |
| **Census**                  | An official count of a population                                  |
| **Demography**              | The study of populations and their characteristics                 |

---

## 2. Population Collocations

A **collocation** is a combination of words that commonly occur together in natural English.

The Ethiopian freshman module specifically asks students to identify words that **co-occur with “population”** and emphasizes learning vocabulary through collocations.

### Common collocations with **population**

| Collocation                 | Example                                                              |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **population growth**       | Rapid population growth can increase demand for housing.             |
| **population decline**      | The region experienced population decline.                           |
| **population density**      | The city has a high population density.                              |
| **population distribution** | Population distribution varies between regions.                      |
| **population structure**    | The population structure is changing.                                |
| **population size**         | The population size increased significantly.                         |
| **population increase**     | The country recorded a population increase.                          |
| **population decrease**     | The area experienced a population decrease.                          |
| **population growth rate**  | The population growth rate is declining.                             |
| **population pyramid**      | A population pyramid shows age and sex structure.                    |
| **population census**       | The government conducted a population census.                        |
| **urban population**        | The urban population is growing rapidly.                             |
| **rural population**        | A large rural population depends on agriculture.                     |
| **working-age population**  | The working-age population is an important part of the labour force. |
| **elderly population**      | The elderly population may require additional healthcare.            |
| **young population**        | Ethiopia has a relatively young population.                          |
| **total population**        | The total population is estimated to have increased.                 |
| **world population**        | The world population has grown considerably.                         |

## 3. Population Verbs

Certain verbs commonly describe what happens to a population.

### Increase / Growth

* **increase**
* **grow**
* **rise**
* **expand**
* **double**
* **triple**

> The population **increased** rapidly.

> The urban population **grew** significantly.

### Decrease / Decline

* **decrease**
* **decline**
* **fall**
* **drop**
* **shrink**

> The rural population **declined** over the period.

## 4. Describing Population Size

Useful adjectives and phrases include:

* **large population**
* **small population**
* **growing population**
* **declining population**
* **rapidly growing population**
* **densely populated**
* **sparsely populated**
* **high population density**
* **low population density**

### Examples

> Ethiopia has a **large population**.

> Some areas are **densely populated**.

> Remote areas may be **sparsely populated**.

---

## 5. Describing Population Change

Use these expressions when describing trends:

| Expression             | Meaning                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **increase rapidly**   | grow quickly                     |
| **increase gradually** | grow slowly                      |
| **rise significantly** | increase noticeably              |
| **remain stable**      | stay approximately the same      |
| **decline gradually**  | decrease slowly                  |
| **fall sharply**       | decrease quickly                 |
| **fluctuate**          | repeatedly increase and decrease |

### Example

> The population **increased gradually** between 2000 and 2010.

> The population **remained relatively stable** after 2015.

---

## 6. Population and Migration Vocabulary

### Migration

**Migration** = movement of people from one place to another.

### Immigration

> **Immigrate to** a country

> Many people **immigrated to** the city.

### Emigration

> **Emigrate from** a country

> Some people **emigrated from** rural areas.

### Other useful terms

* **migrant** → a person who moves
* **rural-to-urban migration** → movement from rural areas to cities
* **internal migration** → movement within a country
* **international migration** → movement between countries

---

## 7. Urban and Rural Population

### Urban population

People living in **towns and cities**.

Common collocations:

* **urban population**
* **urban growth**
* **urbanization**
* **urban migration**
* **urban area**
* **urban centre**

### Rural population

People living in **countryside or rural areas**.

Common collocations:

* **rural population**
* **rural area**
* **rural community**
* **rural development**
* **rural migration**

### Example

> **Urban population** is increasing as people move from rural areas to cities.

---

## 8. Population Density Vocabulary

| Expression                   | Meaning                                               |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **high population density**  | Many people per unit of land                          |
| **low population density**   | Few people per unit of land                           |
| **densely populated**        | Having many people in a relatively small area         |
| **sparsely populated**       | Having relatively few people across an area           |
| **population concentration** | A large number of people located in a particular area |

### Example

> Addis Ababa is **densely populated** compared with many rural areas.

---

## 9. Population Structure Vocabulary

Population structure describes important characteristics of a population.

Common terms:

* **age structure**
* **sex structure**
* **young population**
* **working-age population**
* **elderly population**
* **dependent population**
* **male population**
* **female population**
* **population pyramid**

### Example

> A population pyramid can show the **age and sex structure** of a population.

---

## 10. Cause-and-Effect Vocabulary

Population topics often require explaining **why populations change**.

### Cause

* **because of**
* **due to**
* **as a result of**
* **caused by**
* **driven by**

### Effect

* **lead to**
* **result in**
* **cause**
* **contribute to**
* **increase**
* **reduce**

### Example

> **Rural-to-urban migration can lead to rapid urban population growth.**

> **High birth rates can contribute to population growth.**

## Quick Check

1. What is a collocation?
   **Answer:** A combination of words that commonly occur together in natural language.

2. Complete: **population + ______**
   **Answer:** Growth, density, distribution, structure, decline, size, etc.

3. What is the difference between immigration and emigration?
   **Answer:** Immigration is moving into a country; emigration is moving out.

4. What does “densely populated” mean?
   **Answer:** An area has many people in a relatively small amount of land.

5. Complete: “The ______ population is increasing as people move to cities.”
   **Answer:** **Urban**

6. Which is correct?
   **A. population dense**
   **B. population density**
   **Answer:** **B**

7. What does “working-age population” refer to?
   **Answer:** People generally within the ages considered capable of participating in the workforce.
