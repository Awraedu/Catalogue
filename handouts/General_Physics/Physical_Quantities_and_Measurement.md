# Physical Quantities and Measurement

Measurement is the process of associating numbers with physical quantities and phenomena.

## 1. Physical Quantities
A physical quantity is a property of a material or system that can be quantified by measurement.

- **Base Quantities**: Length, Mass, Time, Electric Current, Temperature, Amount of Substance, Luminous Intensity.
- **Derived Quantities**: Area, Volume, Velocity, Acceleration, Force, etc.

## 2. SI Units
The International System of Units (SI) is the modern form of the metric system.

### Base Units
| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
| -------- | ---- | ------ |
| Length   | meter| m      |
| Mass     | kilogram| kg  |
| Time     | second| s     |
