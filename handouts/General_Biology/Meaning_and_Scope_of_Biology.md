# Meaning and Scope of Biology

Biology is the **scientific study of life**. It is a natural science with a broad scope but has several unifying themes that tie it together as a single, coherent field.

## 1. Defining Life
Life is a characteristic that distinguishes physical entities that have biological processes, such as signaling and self-sustaining processes, from those that do not.

### Characteristics of Life
* **Organization**: Being composed of one or more cells.
* **Metabolism**: Conversion of chemicals and energy into cellular components.
* **Homeostasis**: Regulation of the internal environment.
* **Growth**: Increase in size or number of cells.
* **Reproduction**: The ability to produce new individual organisms.

## 2. Mathematical Modeling in Biology
Modern biology often uses mathematics to describe population growth or chemical reactions.

### The Logistic Growth Formula (LaTeX Test)
The growth of a population can be modeled using the following LaTeX formula:

$$
\frac{dN}{dt} = rN \left(1 - \frac{N}{K}\right)
$$

Where:
- $N$ is population size
- $t$ is time
- $r$ is the intrinsic rate of increase
- $K$ is the carrying capacity
