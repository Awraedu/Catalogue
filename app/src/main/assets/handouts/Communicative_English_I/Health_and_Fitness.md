# Health and Fitness

> **Core idea:** **Health and fitness** involve maintaining physical and mental well-being through healthy habits such as regular physical activity, balanced nutrition, adequate sleep, and stress management.

## 1. What Is Health?

**Health** is a state of overall physical, mental, and social well-being.

Good health involves more than simply being free from illness. It also includes:

* Physical well-being
* Mental and emotional well-being
* Healthy social relationships
* Healthy daily habits

---

## 2. What Is Fitness?

**Physical fitness** is the ability of the body to perform daily activities effectively without excessive fatigue.

Important components include:

| Component        | Meaning                                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Strength**     | Ability of muscles to produce force                  |
| **Endurance**    | Ability to continue an activity for a period of time |
| **Flexibility**  | Ability to move joints through a suitable range      |
| **Balance**      | Ability to maintain body stability                   |
| **Coordination** | Ability to control body movements effectively        |

---

## 3. Benefits of Regular Exercise

Regular physical activity can improve:

### 🏃 Physical Health

* Heart and lung function
* Muscle strength
* Bone health
* Physical endurance
* Energy levels

### 🧠 Mental Well-being

* Mood
* Concentration
* Stress management
* Sleep quality
* General sense of well-being

### 🎓 Academic Performance

For students, regular physical activity can support:

* Better concentration
* Reduced stress
* Improved energy
* Better sleep
* Healthy daily routines

---

## 4. Healthy Lifestyle Habits

A healthy lifestyle includes several connected habits:

### 🥗 Balanced Nutrition

Eat a variety of nutritious foods and maintain appropriate eating habits.

### 💤 Adequate Sleep

Good sleep supports physical recovery, concentration, and learning.

### 🏃 Regular Physical Activity

Include movement and exercise in your daily routine.

### 💧 Hydration

Drink enough fluids to support normal body functions.

### 🧘 Stress Management

Use healthy ways to manage stress, such as exercise, relaxation, social support, and good time management.

---

## 5. Types of Exercise

### Aerobic Exercise

Improves cardiovascular endurance.

**Examples:** walking, running, cycling, swimming.

### Strength Exercise

Builds or maintains muscle strength.

**Examples:** resistance exercises, bodyweight exercises, weight training.

### Flexibility Exercise

Helps maintain range of motion.

**Examples:** stretching and mobility exercises.

### Balance Exercise

Improves stability and body control.

**Examples:** balance drills and controlled single-leg exercises.

---

## 6. Health and Fitness for Students

Students can support their health by:

* Staying physically active
* Eating balanced meals
* Getting enough sleep
* Taking breaks from prolonged sitting
* Managing academic stress
* Maintaining good personal hygiene
* Avoiding harmful habits

> **Good health supports effective learning.**
