# Present Simple and Past Simple

> **Core idea:** The **Present Simple** describes things that are generally true, happen regularly, or are permanent situations. The **Past Simple** describes actions, events, or situations that were completed in the past.

## 1. Present Simple

### Form

> **Subject + base verb**

For **he, she, and it**, add **-s** or **-es**.

| Subject             | Example             |
| ------------------- | ------------------- |
| I / You / We / They | They **work** hard. |
| He / She / It       | She **works** hard. |

### Examples

> I **study** at university.

> She **lives** in Addis Ababa.

> They **play** football every weekend.

---

## 2. Uses of the Present Simple

The Ethiopian freshman module identifies the Present Simple as being used for **repeated actions, long-term facts, and facts that are always true**.

### A. Habits and routines

Use it for actions that happen regularly.

> I **go** to class every morning.

> He **plays** football on Sundays.

Common expressions:

* every day
* every week
* usually
* often
* sometimes
* always
* rarely
* never

---

### B. General truths and facts

> Water **boils** at 100°C.

> The Earth **moves** around the Sun.

> Addis Ababa **is** the capital of Ethiopia.

These statements are generally or always true.

---

### C. Long-term or permanent situations

> She **lives** in Hawassa.

> He **works** at a university.

> They **speak** Amharic.

The focus is on a situation that is generally true, rather than a temporary event.

---

### D. Repeated actions

> The bus **leaves** at 7:00 every morning.

> Students **attend** lectures regularly.

> My brother **calls** me every weekend.

---

# 3. Present Simple: Third-Person -s

With **he, she, and it**, the verb usually takes **-s**.

> I work → She **works**

> I play → He **plays**

> I read → She **reads**

### Add -es

For many verbs ending in **-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o**:

> wash → **washes**

> watch → **watches**

> go → **goes**

> fix → **fixes**

### Consonant + y

Change **y → ies**:

> study → **studies**

> carry → **carries**

But:

> play → **plays**

because there is a vowel before **y**.

---

# 4. Present Simple Negative

Use:

> **do not / does not + base verb**

### I / You / We / They

> I **do not work** on Sundays.

> They **don't study** at night.

### He / She / It

> She **does not work** on Sundays.

> He **doesn't study** at night.

> **Important:** After **doesn't**, use the **base verb**.

❌ She doesn't **works**.

✅ She doesn't **work**.

---

# 5. Present Simple Questions

Use:

> **Do / Does + subject + base verb?**

### Examples

> **Do** you study English?

> **Do** they live here?

> **Does** she study English?

> **Does** he work at the university?

Again, use the **base verb** after *does*.

❌ Does she **works**?

✅ Does she **work**?

---

# 6. Past Simple

The Past Simple describes an action or situation that **finished in the past**.

### Regular verbs

Usually add **-ed**:

> work → worked

> play → played

> visit → visited

### Examples

> I **visited** Gondar last year.

> She **finished** her assignment yesterday.

> They **played** football last Saturday.

---

# 7. Irregular Past Verbs

Many common verbs do not use **-ed**.

| Base  | Past         |
| ----- | ------------ |
| go    | **went**     |
| come  | **came**     |
| see   | **saw**      |
| eat   | **ate**      |
| take  | **took**     |
| write | **wrote**    |
| buy   | **bought**   |
| make  | **made**     |
| have  | **had**      |
| do    | **did**      |
| be    | **was/were** |

These forms need to be memorized.

---

# 8. Uses of the Past Simple

The FLEn 1011 module describes the Past Simple as expressing **finished actions in the past** and **past situations or habits**.

### A. Finished past actions

> I **visited** Adama last week.

> She **finished** the assignment yesterday.

> They **arrived** two hours ago.

The action is completely finished.

---

### B. Past situations

> When I was a child, I **lived** in a small house.

> My father **worked** in a bank.

> We **were** very happy.

These situations existed in the past but are not necessarily true now.

---

### C. Past habits

> When I was young, I **played** football every day.

> We **visited** our grandparents every summer.

The action happened repeatedly in the past.

---

# 9. Past Simple Time Expressions

The Past Simple commonly appears with definite past-time expressions.

| Expression       | Example                                 |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| yesterday        | I went there **yesterday**.             |
| last week        | She arrived **last week**.              |
| last year        | They moved **last year**.               |
| ago              | He left two hours **ago**.              |
| in 2020          | I graduated **in 2020**.                |
| when I was young | I played football **when I was young**. |
| on Monday        | We met **on Monday**.                   |

> **Exam clue:** A **specific finished past time** strongly suggests the **Past Simple**.

---

# 10. Past Simple Negative

Use:

> **did not + base verb**

### Examples

> I **did not go** to class.

> She **didn't finish** the assignment.

> They **didn't play** football.

### Important

After **didn't**, use the **base verb**.

❌ He didn't **went**.

✅ He didn't **go**.

---

# 11. Past Simple Questions

Use:

> **Did + subject + base verb?**

### Examples

> **Did** you visit Gondar?

> **Did** she finish her work?

> **Did** they attend the lecture?

Again, use the **base verb**.

❌ Did you **went**?

✅ Did you **go**?

---

# 12. Present Simple vs. Past Simple

| Present Simple              | Past Simple                  |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| General/regular situation   | Finished past situation      |
| Habits and routines         | Past actions/events          |
| General truths              | Past facts                   |
| Long-term situations        | Past situations              |
| **I work** every day.       | **I worked** yesterday.      |
| **She lives** here.         | **She lived** there in 2020. |
| **They play** every Sunday. | **They played** last Sunday. |

### Easy question

Ask:

> **Is it generally true or repeated now?**

→ **Present Simple**

> **Is it finished and in the past?**

→ **Past Simple**

---

# 13. Compare the Meaning

### Example 1

> I **live** in Addis Ababa.

This describes my current situation.

> I **lived** in Addis Ababa in 2020.

This describes a past situation.

---

### Example 2

> She **plays** football every weekend.

This is a current habit.

> She **played** football last weekend.

This refers to a completed past event.

---

### Example 3

> He **works** at a university.

This is his current employment.

> He **worked** at a university ten years ago.

This was true in the past.

---

# 14. Present Simple vs. Past Simple in a Timeline

### Present Simple

```text
PAST ───────── NOW ───────── FUTURE
              ↑
       regular / general
```

It describes what is generally true or regularly happens.

### Past Simple

```text
PAST                 NOW
  ●──────────────────│
finished action
```

The action happened and finished before now.

---

# 15. A Common Exam Problem

Consider:

> I **have visited** Gondar before.

This is **Present Perfect** because it describes an experience without specifying when.

But:

> I **visited** Gondar **last year**.

This is **Past Simple** because the time is specific and finished.

And:

> I **visit** Gondar every summer.

This is **Present Simple** because it describes a repeated habit.

### Compare all three

| Sentence                          | Tense           | Meaning             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------- |
| I **visit** Gondar every summer.  | Present Simple  | Regular habit       |
| I **visited** Gondar last year.   | Past Simple     | Finished past event |
| I **have visited** Gondar before. | Present Perfect | Life experience     |

---

# 16. Common Mistakes

### ❌ She work at a hospital.

### ✅ She **works** at a hospital.

---

### ❌ He doesn't works here.

### ✅ He doesn't **work** here.

---

### ❌ Did you went to class?

### ✅ Did you **go** to class?

---

### ❌ They didn't played football.

### ✅ They didn't **play** football.

---

### ❌ I go to Adama yesterday.

### ✅ I **went** to Adama yesterday.

---

### ❌ I visited him every day now.

### ✅ I **visit** him every day.

# ⭐ Exam Focus

The Ethiopian freshman module asks students to distinguish the four major tenses by **meaning and context**, not simply by memorizing their forms.

### Present Simple

> **Habit + routine + general truth + long-term fact**

**Form:**

> **Subject + V1 (+ s/es)**

### Past Simple

> **Finished past action + past situation + past habit**

**Form:**

> **Subject + V2**

### Negative

> Present: **do/does + not + V1**

> Past: **did + not + V1**

### Questions

> Present: **Do/Does + subject + V1?**

> Past: **Did + subject + V1?**

### Fast exam rule

> **Every day / usually / often → Present Simple**

> **Yesterday / last year / ago / in 2020 → Past Simple**

But always consider the **meaning**, not just the time expression.

## Quick Check

### 1. Choose the correct answer:

She ______ English every day.

A. study
B. studies
C. studied
D. studying

**Answer: B — studies**

---

### 2. Choose the correct answer:

We ______ football yesterday.

A. play
B. plays
C. played
D. playing

**Answer: C — played**

---

### 3. Correct the sentence:

> He doesn't works on Sunday.

**Answer:**

> He doesn't **work** on Sunday.

---

### 4. Correct the sentence:

> Did she went to class?

**Answer:**

> Did she **go** to class?

---

### 5. Identify the tense:

> I usually take the bus to university.

**Answer:** **Present Simple**

---

### 6. Identify the tense:

> I took the bus yesterday.

**Answer:** **Past Simple**

---

### 7. Which sentence describes a general truth?

A. The students studied yesterday.
B. Water boils at 100°C.
C. She visited Hawassa last year.
D. He went home two hours ago.

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. Which sentence describes a past habit?

A. I play football every week.
B. I played football when I was a child.
C. I am playing football now.
D. I have played football before.

**Answer: B**

---

> **💡 Remember:**
> **Present Simple = what is generally true, regular, or habitual.**
> **Past Simple = what happened and finished in the past.**
>
> **Present:** `do/does + V1`
> **Past:** `did + V1`
> **Past statement:** `V2`
