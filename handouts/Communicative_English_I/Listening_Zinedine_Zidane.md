# Listening: Zinedine Zidane

> **Core idea:** This listening activity develops the ability to **listen to a short biography, take useful notes, and identify specific information** about a person's life and career.

## 1. Who Is Zinedine Zidane?

**Zinedine Zidane** is a famous French former professional football player and football manager. He is widely known for his technical ability, creativity, and achievements in football.

In the Ethiopian freshman English module, the listening activity uses Zidane's life and career to practice **listening and note-taking**.

---

## 2. Key Vocabulary

| Word                 | Meaning                                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Career**           | The work or profession someone does over a period of time |
| **Profession**       | A type of job or occupation                               |
| **Professional**     | Relating to a person's paid occupation or trained work    |
| **Achievement**      | Something successfully accomplished                       |
| **Sportsperson**     | A person who participates in sports                       |
| **Playing position** | The role a player has on a team                           |
| **Record**           | The best or highest achievement officially recorded       |
| **Crowd**            | A large group of people gathered together                 |
| **Jog**              | To run slowly, usually for exercise                       |

### Important distinction

> **Career** = a person's professional journey
> **Profession** = a person's type of occupation
> **Achievement** = something successfully accomplished

---

## 3. Pre-Listening Strategy

Before listening, think about what you already know about the person.

Ask yourself:

* Who is Zidane?
* What sport did he play?
* What country is he associated with?
* What was his position?
* What teams did he play for?
* What achievements is he famous for?

> **Prediction helps you recognize information when you hear it.**

---

## 4. Listen for Specific Information

The Ethiopian freshman activity asks students to listen and complete a table containing important facts about Zidane.

| Information          | What to listen for             |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Full name            | Person's complete name         |
| Date of birth        | Date/year                      |
| Place of birth       | City/country                   |
| Known for            | Main achievement or profession |
| Playing position     | Football position              |
| Played for           | Clubs/teams                    |
| Real Madrid transfer | Transfer fee                   |
| Games won            | Important victories            |
| Special points       | Other notable information      |

### Listening tip

Do **not** try to write every sentence.

Instead, write short notes:

> **Name → Zidane**
> **Born → date/place**
> **Position → football position**
> **Clubs → team names**
> **Transfer → fee**

---

## 5. WH-Questions

The information from the listening can be used to form **WH-questions**.

### Who?

> **Who was Zinedine Zidane?**

### When?

> **When was Zidane born?**

### Where?

> **Where was he born?**

### What?

> **What was his playing position?**

### Which?

> **Which clubs did he play for?**

### How much?

> **How much was his transfer fee to Real Madrid?**

> **WH-questions help you identify exactly what information you need to listen for.**

---

## 6. Note-Taking During Listening

Use abbreviations and keywords instead of complete sentences.

### Example

Instead of writing:

> Zinedine Zidane was a professional football player who played in the midfield position.

Write:

> **Zidane → professional footballer → midfielder**

This allows you to keep listening while recording the important information.

---

## 7. Listen More Than Once

The module asks students to listen several times:

1. **First listening** → take short notes.
2. **Discuss** → compare notes with classmates.
3. **Second listening** → complete the information table.
4. **Final listening** → check and finalize answers.
5. **Post-listening** → use the information to ask and answer questions.

### Best strategy

> **First listen → understand the general topic**
> **Second listen → find specific information**
> **Third listen → check details**

---

## ⭐ Exam Focus

For a biography listening task, concentrate on:

**WHO → WHEN → WHERE → WHAT → WHICH → HOW MUCH**

Especially watch for:

* Names
* Dates
* Places
* Numbers
* Teams
* Occupations
* Achievements
* Transfer fees

### Common question types

> **When was he born?** → Date
> **Where was he born?** → Place
> **What was his profession?** → Occupation
> **What position did he play?** → Position
> **Which team did he play for?** → Team
> **How much was the transfer fee?** → Amount
