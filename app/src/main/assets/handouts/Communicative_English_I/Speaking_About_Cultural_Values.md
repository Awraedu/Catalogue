# Speaking About Cultural Values

> **Core idea:** Speaking about cultural values means expressing ideas about the beliefs, principles, and behaviors that are important to a community, while giving reasons, examples, and personal opinions respectfully.

## 1. What Are Cultural Values?

**Cultural values** are shared principles and ideals that influence how members of a community think, behave, and interact.

Examples may include:

* Respect for elders
* Hospitality
* Cooperation
* Honesty
* Responsibility
* Community support
* Hard work
* Respect for family
* Helping others

The FLEn 1011 module defines cultural values as the **core principles and ideals upon which a community exists**. It asks students to identify cultural values shared within Ethiopian communities.

> **Important:** Ethiopia is culturally diverse, so a value or practice common in one community may not be shared by every Ethiopian.

---

# 2. Why Talk About Cultural Values?

Discussing cultural values helps you:

* Express personal opinions
* Describe your culture
* Explain traditions and beliefs
* Compare cultures respectfully
* Understand different perspectives
* Develop speaking confidence
* Give reasons and examples

In FLEn 1011, the topic is used to develop speaking as well as reading, listening, and writing skills.

---

# 3. Giving Your Opinion

Useful expressions:

### Giving an opinion

> **I think that...**

> **In my opinion,...**

> **I believe that...**

> **From my point of view,...**

> **Personally, I think...**

### Example

> **In my opinion, respect for elders is an important cultural value because it strengthens relationships between generations.**

---

# 4. Agreeing and Disagreeing

### Agreeing

> **I agree with you.**

> **I completely agree.**

> **That's a good point.**

> **I think so too.**

### Partly agreeing

> **I agree to some extent, but...**

> **That's true, although...**

> **I understand your point, but...**

### Disagreeing politely

> **I see it differently.**

> **I'm not sure I agree.**

> **I respect your opinion, but I think...**

> **I understand what you mean; however,...**

> **Good speaking means expressing disagreement respectfully.**

---

# 5. Giving Reasons

An opinion becomes stronger when you explain **why**.

Useful connectors:

* because
* since
* as
* therefore
* so
* this is because

### Example

> I value **hospitality because** it encourages people to welcome and support others.

> Respect for elders is important **because** older people can provide experience and guidance.

### Pattern

> **Opinion + because + reason**

---

# 6. Giving Examples

Examples make your ideas clearer.

Useful expressions:

> **For example,...**

> **For instance,...**

> **Such as...**

> **One example is...**

### Example

> Cooperation is an important value. **For example**, people may work together to help a neighbor during a difficult time.

### Speaking pattern

> **Point → Reason → Example**

---

# 7. Talking About Personal Values

You may be asked:

> **Which cultural value are you most proud of?**

A strong answer should include:

1. The value
2. Why it is important
3. An example
4. Its effect on people

### Example

> **I am proud of the value of hospitality. I think it is important because it encourages people to welcome and support others. For example, guests are often offered food or drink and treated warmly. This value can strengthen relationships within a community.**

---

# 8. Describing Cultural Values

Useful sentence patterns:

> **One important cultural value is...**

> **This value is important because...**

> **People show this value by...**

> **An example of this value is...**

> **This tradition helps to...**

### Example

> **One important cultural value is cooperation. People show this value by helping one another during community activities. This strengthens social relationships.**

---

# 9. Talking About Change

Cultural values can change over time.

Useful expressions:

> **In the past,...**

> **Today,...**

> **Nowadays,...**

> **Compared with the past,...**

> **Cultural attitudes are changing because...**

### Example

> In the past, some cultural practices were followed more strictly. **Today, younger generations may interpret them differently because of education, technology, and increased interaction with other cultures.**

---

# 10. Discussing Positive and Negative Aspects

Not every cultural practice should automatically be accepted or rejected.

### Positive

> This value helps communities cooperate.

> It strengthens family relationships.

> It promotes respect and social responsibility.

### Critical perspective

> Some practices may need to change when they cause harm.

> A tradition can be culturally important while still being open to discussion.

### Useful expression

> **I respect the cultural value, but I think the practice should be reconsidered because...**

This allows you to discuss culture **critically and respectfully**.

---

# 11. Comparing Cultural Values

Useful expressions:

### Similarity

> **Both cultures value...**

> **Similarly,...**

> **In both communities,...**

### Difference

> **However,...**

> **In contrast,...**

> **Unlike...**

> **One difference is that...**

### Example

> Both communities value cooperation. **However**, they may express this value through different traditions.

---

# 12. Asking Others About Cultural Values

Useful questions:

> **What cultural value is important to you?**

> **Why do you value it?**

> **How do people demonstrate this value?**

> **Is this value common in your community?**

> **Has this value changed over time?**

> **Do young people have the same cultural values as older generations?**

> **Are there any cultural practices you think should change? Why?**

---

# 13. Responding to Questions

Avoid answers that are only:

> “Yes.”

> “No.”

> “I don't know.”

Try to **extend your answer**.

### Weak

> Do you value hospitality?

> Yes.

### Better

> Yes, I do. I think hospitality is important because it helps people feel welcome and strengthens social relationships.

### Easy formula

> **Answer → Reason → Example**

---

# 14. A Useful Speaking Structure

When discussing a cultural value, use:

> **V → R → E → E**

### **V — Value**

What is the value?

### **R — Reason**

Why is it important?

### **E — Example**

How is it demonstrated?

### **E — Effect**

What positive effect does it have?

### Example

> **Value:** Respect for elders
>
> **Reason:** Older people can provide experience and guidance.
>
> **Example:** Younger people may listen carefully to advice from older family members.
>
> **Effect:** It can strengthen relationships between generations.

---

# 15. Short Speaking Model

### Question:

> **What cultural value are you proud of?**

### Answer:

> **I am proud of the value of cooperation. I believe it is important because people can achieve more when they help one another. For example, community members may work together to solve a local problem. Cooperation creates stronger relationships and a sense of responsibility.**

Notice the structure:

> **Opinion → Reason → Example → Effect**

---

# 16. Cultural Values and Respect

When discussing culture, remember:

> **Different does not necessarily mean wrong.**

People may have different:

* Beliefs
* Traditions
* Languages
* Food
* Clothing
* Family practices
* Ways of celebrating
* Social expectations

Use respectful language when comparing them.

### Instead of:

> “Their culture is strange.”

Say:

> “Their cultural practices are different from those I am familiar with.”

### Instead of:

> “Our culture is better.”

Say:

> “I value this practice because it is important to my community.”

# 17. Useful Vocabulary

| Word          | Meaning                                                  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Culture**   | Shared way of life of a group                            |
| **Value**     | Principle considered important                           |
| **Tradition** | Practice passed from generation to generation            |
| **Belief**    | An idea accepted as true                                 |
| **Custom**    | Common traditional practice                              |
| **Norm**      | Expected behavior in a society                           |
| **Heritage**  | Cultural traditions and features inherited from the past |
| **Respect**   | Showing consideration for others                         |
| **Community** | People living or functioning together as a group         |
| **Diversity** | Variety among people or cultures                         |

# 18. Common Speaking Mistakes

### ❌ Giving only an opinion

> “Hospitality is important.”

### ✅ Give a reason

> “Hospitality is important **because** it helps people feel welcome.”

---

### ❌ Giving an example without explaining it

> “People help their neighbors.”

### ✅ Explain the connection

> “People help their neighbors, **which demonstrates the value of cooperation**.”

---

### ❌ Making generalizations

> “All Ethiopians believe the same thing.”

### ✅ Be specific

> “This value is common in some Ethiopian communities.”

---

### ❌ Criticizing another culture

> “Their tradition is strange.”

### ✅ Express your perspective respectfully

> “This practice is different from what I am familiar with.”

## ⭐ Exam Focus

For a speaking question about cultural values, remember:

> **STATE → EXPLAIN → EXAMPLE → EFFECT**

### Example

**State:**

> One cultural value I appreciate is hospitality.

**Explain:**

> I think it is important because it encourages people to welcome others.

**Example:**

> For example, guests may be offered food and drink.

**Effect:**

> This can strengthen relationships and create a sense of community.

## Quick Check

### 1. What are cultural values?

**Answer:** Shared principles and ideals that influence how members of a community think and behave.

### 2. How can you make your opinion stronger?

**Answer:** Give a reason and an example.

### 3. Complete:

> I value hospitality ______ it helps people feel welcome.

**Answer:** **because**

### 4. Which is more respectful?

A. “Their culture is strange.”
B. “Their cultural practices are different from those I am familiar with.”

**Answer: B**

### 5. Give a polite way to disagree.

**Answer:** “I understand your point, but I see it differently.”

### 6. What should you include when answering “What cultural value are you proud of?”

**Answer:** The value, why it is important, an example, and its effect.

### 7. Complete the speaking structure:

> Opinion → ______ → Example → Effect

**Answer:** **Reason**
