# Goals of Psychology

Psychology seeks to understand behavior and mental processes through four primary goals.

## 1. Description
The first goal is to name or classify behavior as objectively as possible.
- *Question*: What is happening?

## 2. Explanation
Understanding *why* a behavior or mental process occurred. This often involves identifying causes.
- *Question*: Why is it happening?

## 3. Prediction
Specifying the conditions under which a behavior or event will likely occur.
- *Question*: When will it happen again?

## 4. Control (Change)
Applying psychological knowledge to prevent unwanted behavior or bring about desired outcomes.
- *Question*: How can we change or influence it?
