# Africa's Wild Animals

> **Core idea:** Africa is home to some of the world's most diverse and remarkable wildlife, including large mammals, birds, reptiles, and many other species.

---

## 1. Africa's Wildlife

Africa has a great variety of **wild animals** because the continent contains many different environments, including:

* Grasslands and savannas
* Forests
* Deserts
* Mountains
* Wetlands
* Rivers and lakes

Different animals are adapted to different environments.

For example:

> **Lions** are commonly associated with grasslands and savannas, while **gorillas** live mainly in tropical forests.

---

## 2. The Big Five

The term **"Big Five"** originally referred to five large African animals that were considered difficult and dangerous to hunt.

Today, the term is mainly used in **wildlife tourism and conservation**.

The Big Five are:

| Animal            | Key characteristic                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 🦁 **Lion**       | A large social predator           |
| 🐘 **Elephant**   | The largest land animal           |
| 🐆 **Leopard**    | A powerful and adaptable predator |
| 🦏 **Rhinoceros** | Large herbivore with a horn       |
| 🐃 **Buffalo**    | Strong herd-living herbivore      |

> **Important:** "Big Five" does not simply mean the five biggest animals in Africa.

---

## 3. Herbivores and Carnivores

Animals can be classified according to what they eat.

### Herbivores

**Herbivores** mainly eat plants.

Examples:

* Elephant
* Giraffe
* Zebra
* Rhinoceros
* Buffalo

### Carnivores

**Carnivores** mainly eat other animals.

Examples:

* Lion
* Leopard
* Cheetah
* Hyena

### Omnivores

**Omnivores** eat both plants and animals.

Examples include some species of:

* Baboons
* Monkeys
* Wild pigs

---

## 4. Adaptation

An **adaptation** is a feature or behavior that helps an animal survive in its environment.

### Examples

**Elephant**

→ Large ears help release heat.

**Giraffe**

→ A long neck helps it reach leaves high in trees.

**Cheetah**

→ A streamlined body and powerful legs help it run at high speed.

**Camel**

→ Special adaptations help it survive in dry desert environments.

> **Adaptation = a feature or behavior that helps an organism survive.**

---

## 5. Predators and Prey

A **predator** is an animal that hunts another animal for food.

The animal being hunted is called **prey**.

### Example

```text
Lion → Zebra
predator   prey
```

Predators and prey are connected through **food chains**.

### Simple food chain

```text
Grass → Zebra → Lion
```

The grass provides energy to the zebra, and the zebra provides energy to the lion.

---

## 6. Wildlife and the Environment

Wild animals are an important part of ecosystems.

They help maintain ecological balance through:

* Feeding relationships
* Pollination
* Seed dispersal
* Population control
* Nutrient cycling

For example, predators can help control populations of other animals, while some animals help spread seeds as they move through their environment.

---

## 7. Threats to African Wildlife

Many wild animals face serious threats.

Major threats include:

* **Habitat loss** — natural habitats are destroyed or reduced.
* **Poaching** — animals are illegally hunted.
* **Climate change** — changing conditions affect habitats and food sources.
* **Pollution** — harmful substances damage ecosystems.
* **Human-wildlife conflict** — people and animals compete for land and resources.

### Conservation

**Conservation** means protecting nature, wildlife, and natural resources.

Conservation efforts include:

* National parks
* Wildlife reserves
* Anti-poaching programs
* Habitat protection
* Breeding and recovery programs
* Environmental education
